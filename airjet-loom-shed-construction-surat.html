Technical Points

Simple Details That Matter On An Airjet Shed

These choices affect how the shed works every day.

  • Clean environment: Lint and dust can affect weaving. Openings, finishes and cleaning access are discussed early.
  • Compressed air: Lines follow the rows. Size and pressure come from the machine supplier.
  • Humidity and temperature: The target range belongs to your process, and the enclosure is built around it.
  • Level floor: A consistent floor helps installation and movement. Joints and finishes are agreed before casting.
  • Manageable vibration: Supports and floor thickness follow supplier notes so machines stay steady.
  • Power distribution: Cable routes and distribution points are planned before flooring.
  • Orderly rows: Straight, well-spaced rows help operators, trolleys and maintenance.
